# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first public release.

- This Week: live scores and projections from the NFL game clock, and a strip of the week's top scores.
- Matchup pages: both lineups with each player's game, clock, stats (catches in PPR leagues), injury tag, points and projection, plus win probability.
- Standings with divisions and the playoff line; the playoff picture, then the bracket with byes and the third-place game.
- Scores: every finished week, weekly high scores and the total points race.
- Money page (optional): dues before the season (entry and keeper fees), then a settle-up after it (pickup and trade fees against weekly and season prizes). Dollar or percent prizes, payments and who owes what. Leagues can also settle everything at the end.
- League page: ESPN's rules plus your own, keepers with draft-round tags, and league history from ESPN.
- Draft season: countdown, draft order and keepers once the league is renewed.
- Commissioner Tools: record payments, paste new ESPN cookies and update now, behind a passcode.
- Private leagues through ESPN cookies, tested before they're saved. An email to you if updates stop working.
- Everything set from the spreadsheet: the League Hub menu and the Settings tab (colors, logo, names, rules, money, update speed).
- Hosted by Apps Script, or on any web host with the self-hosted page.
